# Autonomy Bridge Enquiry Privacy Notice

This notice explains how I handle information you provide when you contact Autonomy Bridge about potential work. I’m Deepak Gupta, and I am responsible for handling these enquiries.

### Information you provide

The contact form asks for your name, email address, organization and message. You may also select the type of work you want to discuss. Please leave out confidential customer, donor or employee information.

### How I use your information

I use the information you submit only to respond to your enquiry and discuss potential work. I do not use your enquiry details for newsletters or unrelated marketing.

### Who handles enquiries

I personally read and handle enquiries. I do not delegate access to an assistant or contractor.

### How long I keep unsuccessful enquiries

If an enquiry does not lead to an engagement, I normally delete its records 12 months after our last substantive contact, unless a legal requirement requires longer retention. If we begin an engagement, we establish the information-handling arrangements for that work separately.

### Questions and requests

You can use the contact form to ask how your enquiry information is handled, request access or correction, or ask for deletion. Identify your message as a privacy request. I may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request. Any applicable retention requirements still apply.
