A practical analytical checklist for warehouse automation business cases, cash flows, demand assumptions and decision ownership.

## Build the buyer’s cash-flow case

Separate theoretical labor productivity from avoidable payroll cost. Estimate installed investment, incremental operating costs and other cash benefits over the buyer’s chosen horizon. Use the Robotics ROI Model for the distinction between payback time and horizon return.

## Test demand and implementation assumptions

Use the facility’s demand history, task mix and operating schedule. Assess charging, workstation, integration and ramp constraints. Do not infer that halving utilization doubles payback: the result depends on the modeled net-cash-flow function.

## Clarify approval and delivery responsibilities

Identify who validates the operational assumptions, owns the business case and approves expenditure. Document acceptance criteria, service obligations and escalation paths. Publicly unsupported company metrics and general labor-displacement percentages have been withdrawn; they are not benchmarks for a proposal.
