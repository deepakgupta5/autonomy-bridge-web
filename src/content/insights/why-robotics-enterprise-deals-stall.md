An analytical guide to diagnosing robotics procurement delays, evidence gaps and decision ownership.

## Map the unresolved decision

Identify the stakeholders responsible for operations, finance, procurement, security and implementation. Ask what each needs to decide and which evidence is missing. A prolonged opportunity does not by itself identify the cause of delay.

## Distinguish technical and commercial evidence

A pilot may answer an operational question without establishing a business case, approval path or implementation owner. Define the intended decision before collecting further pilot evidence. No universal sales-cycle length or conversion rate is asserted here.

## Evaluate interventions without promising an outcome

Consider parallel stakeholder work, a clearer financial case or explicit decision criteria where the opportunity evidence supports them. Track outcomes against a defined baseline. Unsupported claims of quantified improvement, company counts and generalized commercial causation have been withdrawn.
