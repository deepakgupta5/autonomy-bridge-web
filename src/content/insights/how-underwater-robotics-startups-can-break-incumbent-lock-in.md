Questions for evaluating underwater robotics procurement, integration, delivery capacity and routes to market.

## Examine the buyer’s switching requirements

Map the existing equipment, software, operating procedures and service relationships that a replacement would affect. Ask the buyer which requirements are contractual or operational and what evidence a new supplier would need to provide. Do not assume that technical performance alone explains a purchasing decision.

## Separate demand from delivery capacity

Distinguish an expression of interest, a trial, a purchase order and a repeatable delivery commitment. Model manufacturing, deployment and support capacity against the specific contracts under consideration. Unverified company funding, booking and customer claims have been withdrawn from this account.

## Evaluate a route to market

Compare direct sales, an integration partner and a specialist service offer using the target buyer’s procurement requirements. Test the commercial hypothesis through buyer interviews and documented opportunity evidence; this page does not establish an industry sales-cycle benchmark.
