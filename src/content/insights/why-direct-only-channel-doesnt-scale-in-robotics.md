An analytical comparison of direct and partner-led robotics sales, delivery responsibilities and channel economics.

## Identify the constraint before choosing a channel

Examine whether a sales or delivery constraint concerns buyer access, technical integration, local support, procurement requirements or internal capacity. A partner may address a specific constraint, but channel expansion does not guarantee revenue growth.

## Compare the operating models

Model direct and partner-led offers using their actual acquisition, training, integration, support and working-capital requirements. Specify which party owns each obligation and how the parties are paid. Do not apply an unsourced standard channel margin or conversion uplift.

## Define a limited evaluation

Agree on target opportunities, responsibilities and evidence needed to assess the relationship. Review results against the agreed scope. The analysis does not establish that a particular channel is necessary for every robotics company.
