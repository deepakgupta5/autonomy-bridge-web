Questions for distinguishing maritime autonomy contract scope, repeatability, delivery obligations and revenue assumptions.

## Identify what the reported measure means

Distinguish an announced agreement, a contract value, backlog, recognized revenue and cash received. Record the period, scope and source for any comparison. Unsupported company totals have been withdrawn rather than treated as interchangeable measures.

## Examine delivery and repeatability

Assess whether the proposed work is a one-off project, a repeat service or a continuing contract. Identify vessel, staffing, maintenance, regulatory and data-delivery responsibilities for the specific operation. Do not infer a sustainable business model from an announced contract alone.

## Test the commercial interpretation

Compare the assumptions behind expected revenue with delivery capacity, costs and contract terms. Treat conclusions about revenue quality as hypotheses requiring transaction-specific evidence, not as a verified ranking of named vendors.
