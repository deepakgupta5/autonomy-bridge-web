Analytical questions about exoskeleton buyer budgets, evaluation requirements and distribution economics.

## Understand the purchasing category

Evaluate the initial installed investment (C0, currency units) against incremental net cash flows over a stated horizon H (years). Define utilization using throughput and capacity over the same period. A break-even utilization or volume can be solved only if the project-specific cash-flow function supports one; it is not a universal operating threshold. Annual capital cost divided by savings per unit has units of units per year, not a utilization percentage, and does not by itself establish project payback.

## Examine channel incentives

Compare the support, training, inventory and evaluation work required of a distributor with the proposed commercial terms. Model the economics of a particular relationship; no company-count, margin or channel-conversion benchmark is established here.

## Test adoption in context

Define the task, user needs and assessment criteria before an evaluation. Distinguish user acceptance, purchasing approval and sustained use. Company-specific commercial claims without publishable support have been removed; the remaining questions are analytical propositions, not measured sector findings.
