# What Autonomous Vehicle Companies Should Examine in Freight Sales

An analytical checklist for relating autonomous freight proposals to route economics, operating requirements and procurement.

## Start with the freight operation

Specify the route, schedule, payload, service requirements and exception handling that the proposed system must support. Compare the complete operating model with the buyer’s current alternative. A successful demonstration is a different evidence item from an enforceable freight contract.

## Make the economic comparison explicit

Include vehicle and infrastructure costs, remote assistance, maintenance, insurance, staffing and operating constraints where applicable. Use route-specific assumptions and record their sources. No company revenue or sector-wide funding figure is established by this analysis.

## Qualify the buying decision

Identify the operational sponsor, budget authority, risk review and procurement requirements. Define what evidence would allow each party to make a decision. Treat a proposed commercial bottleneck as a hypothesis; do not infer causation from fundraising or company closures alone.

## Evidence and scope of this revision

This page presents analytical questions. The previous company-specific figures, empirical benchmarks and causal attributions did not have publishable supporting records in this repository and have been withdrawn. No new market measurements or client results are asserted.
