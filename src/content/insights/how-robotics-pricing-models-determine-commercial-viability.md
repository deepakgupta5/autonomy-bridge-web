An analytical guide to comparing robotics pricing with buyer budgets, approval paths and vendor cash-flow requirements.

## Match the commercial offer to the buying process

Identify the budget owner, approval authority and contract requirements for the proposed purchase. Compare outright purchase, lease, subscription and outcome-based options against those constraints. A pricing structure should be evaluated alongside its total cost and allocation of responsibility; changing the structure does not establish a conversion-rate improvement.

## Compare cash flows on both sides

For the buyer, model payments and incremental operating costs over a common horizon. For the vendor, model hardware acquisition, financing, service obligations, renewal assumptions and residual value. A recurring revenue contract does not by itself establish profitable unit economics.

## Test the proposed explanation

Ask whether a stalled purchase reflects an unaffordable price, an unavailable budget category, an incomplete business case or an operational concern. Treat pricing mismatch as a hypothesis to test with the buyer, not a measured explanation for a stated share of robotics companies.
