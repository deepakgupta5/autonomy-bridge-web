A deployment and financing model in which warehouse operators pay subscription or usage-based fees for robotic systems rather than purchasing hardware outright, converting capital expenditure to operational expenditure.

Compare contract payments, service scope, ownership, residual value and termination obligations with the purchase alternative over the same horizon. Risk allocation depends on the contract; recurring pricing alone does not establish lower cost or transfer all utilization risk.
