The avoided cost of seasonal hiring, overtime premiums, and agency fees that represents the primary ROI numerator in retail distribution center automation.

Estimate costs that the proposed system would actually avoid during peak periods, including temporary staffing and overtime where applicable. Compare them with incremental investment and operating costs over a common horizon. Avoided hours are not automatically cash savings.
