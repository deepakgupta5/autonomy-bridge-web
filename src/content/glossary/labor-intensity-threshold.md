A project-specific assessment of whether avoidable labor costs contribute enough cash benefit to support an investment over a stated horizon. No labor-cost-to-volume ratio alone establishes a go/no-go decision. Model installed investment, avoidable cash benefits, incremental operating costs and the chosen horizon together.
